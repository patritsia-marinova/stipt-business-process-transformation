# Process Improvement & Solution Design – STIPT

## Overview

During Sprint 2, I built upon the findings from the initial business analysis to design an improved future-state process (SOLL) for STIPT.

My work focused on addressing bottlenecks in planning, scheduling, and complaint handling through digitalisation, clearer communication, and more efficient workflows.

To support the proposed solution, I developed user personas, onboarding journeys, the SOLL process, requirements, a RACI matrix, and a GAP analysis.

The proposed SOLL process was presented to the client and approved.
