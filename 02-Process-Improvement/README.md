# Process Improvement & Solution Design – STIPT

## Overview

During Sprint 2 of the STIPT Business Process Transformation project, I focused on translating the findings from the initial business analysis into a proposed solution for the client.

Building on the IST process, fishbone analysis, and identified bottlenecks and wastes, I worked on redesigning the organisation's planning, scheduling, and complaint-handling processes. The objective was to reduce manual activities, improve communication between stakeholders, and introduce digital solutions that support more efficient operations.

This phase also focused on understanding user needs, preparing employees for the transition to a new system, and defining the requirements and responsibilities necessary to support the proposed improvements.

## Process Improvement Activities

### 1. User Personas

[View User Personas](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/stipt-business-process-transformation/blob/main/02-Process-Improvement/User%20Personas%20Stipt.pdf)

I developed three user personas representing the **Cleaner, Team Leader, and Account Manager**, who are directly involved in the organisation's operational processes.

Each persona explores the user's goals, frustrations, motivations, responsibilities, and technological skills. This allowed me to examine the different challenges employees experience within the current workflow and understand how their needs should influence the proposed digital solution.

The personas helped ensure that the improvements introduced in the SOLL process addressed actual user frustrations rather than being based only on assumptions about how the system should operate.

### 2. User Onboarding Journey

[View User Onboarding Journey](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/stipt-business-process-transformation/blob/main/02-Process-Improvement/Stipt%20onbourding%20journey.odt)

Using the user personas, I designed onboarding journeys for the different roles to understand how employees would be introduced to the proposed digital system.

The journeys map users' actions, emotions, touchpoints, and pain points throughout the transition. This helped identify where users might struggle with unfamiliar functionality, require additional guidance, or benefit from training and support.

By considering the onboarding experience, I was able to look beyond the technical functionality of the solution and address the importance of **user adoption and change management** during business process transformation.

### 3. SOLL – Future Process Design

[View SOLL Process](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/stipt-business-process-transformation/blob/main/02-Process-Improvement/SOLL%20Stipt.pdf)

Based on the findings from the IST analysis, I designed the desired future-state process (SOLL) to address the operational inefficiencies identified during Sprint 1.

The redesigned process proposed several improvements:

- **Digital planning and scheduling:** Introducing a more structured way to coordinate cleaning activities and working hours while reducing unnecessary manual communication.
- **Digital complaint handling:** Improving how customer complaints are received, communicated, investigated, and resolved.
- **Improved communication:** Establishing clearer information flows between cleaners, team leaders, account managers, and customers.
- **Reduced manual activities:** Identifying opportunities to simplify tasks and remove process steps that do not add value.
- **Sustainability:** Incorporating digitalisation, route optimisation, and reduced paper usage into the proposed workflow.

The purpose of the SOLL was not simply to digitalise existing activities but to redesign the workflow around the problems identified in the business analysis.

The final SOLL process was presented to the client and approved as the proposed solution.

### 4. Requirements Analysis

[View STIPT Requirements](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/stipt-business-process-transformation/blob/main/02-Process-Improvement/Stipt%20Requirements%20%281%29.pdf)

After designing the SOLL process, I translated the proposed improvements into requirements to define what the digital system would need to support.

The requirements were developed in a **user-story format**, connecting system functionality to the needs and responsibilities of the different stakeholders.

This helped establish a clear relationship between the identified business problems, the redesigned workflow, and the functionality required from the proposed solution.

By linking requirements directly to the SOLL process, I aimed to ensure that the technical solution would support the organisation's operational needs rather than introducing features without a clear business purpose.

### 5. RACI Matrix – Roles & Responsibilities

[View RACI Matrix](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/stipt-business-process-transformation/blob/main/02-Process-Improvement/Stipt%20RACI.png)

To clarify responsibilities within the redesigned workflow, I developed a RACI matrix identifying which stakeholders are **Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed** for different process activities.

This was particularly important because the IST analysis highlighted communication dependencies and coordination issues between employees and management.

The matrix provided a structured overview of how responsibilities should be distributed within the proposed process, helping reduce ambiguity about task ownership, decision-making, and communication.

### 6. GAP Analysis – IST vs. SOLL

[View GAP Analysis](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/stipt-business-process-transformation/blob/main/02-Process-Improvement/Stipt%20GAP.png)

Finally, I developed a GAP analysis comparing the current-state process (IST) with the proposed future-state process (SOLL).

The analysis identifies the differences between the existing and redesigned workflows, demonstrating how the proposed changes address problems identified during Sprint 1.

The comparison focuses on improvements in communication, reduction of manual work, clearer responsibilities, and overall process efficiency.

This allowed me to connect the initial business analysis to the proposed solution and identify the changes required to move from the current situation to the desired future state.

## Outcome

The main outcome of Sprint 2 was a **client-approved SOLL process**, supported by user personas, onboarding journeys, requirements, a RACI matrix, and GAP analysis.

Through this phase, I developed a stronger understanding of how business process improvement involves more than designing an efficient workflow. It also requires considering user needs, organisational responsibilities, change management, and the technical requirements needed to support the solution.

Together, these deliverables demonstrate my ability to translate business problems into a structured, user-centred process improvement proposal.
