# Implementation & Validation – STIPT

## Overview

During the final sprints of the STIPT Business Process Transformation project, I focused on bringing together the system design, data analysis, and prototyping activities to demonstrate how the proposed solution could work in practice.

Building on the final ERD and KPIs from the previous phase, I developed a SQL database, analysed performance indicators using Python and Pandas, created a Power Apps prototype, and prepared business deliverables to demonstrate the solution's functionality and value.

## Implementation & Validation Activities

### 1. Invoice – Excel

I created an Excel invoice template covering the prototype and Power Apps development work.

Although the invoice was not automatically generated, it demonstrated how the project's development activities could be presented in a structured, professional business document.

### 2. Business Balanced Scorecard (BBSC)

Using the KPIs defined during Sprint 3, I developed a Business Balanced Scorecard to connect operational performance with the organisation's broader business objectives.

The scorecard demonstrates how indicators such as complaint handling and system usage could support management decisions and long-term improvements.

### 3. Factsheet – KPI Analysis & Visualisation

I developed a Factsheet to present and visualise selected KPIs using Python and Pandas.

I prepared the data by checking dates, ratings, and missing values, ensuring that the calculations followed the KPI definitions. The visualisations helped translate the analysis into understandable performance results.

### 4. SQL Database & KPI Validation

Based on the final ERD, I developed SQL CREATE and INSERT statements to structure and populate the database.

I also wrote SQL queries for all five KPIs, including calculations, percentage thresholds, and HIT/NOT HIT evaluations.

This allowed me to validate the database structure and KPI calculations before implementing the analysis in Python.

### 5. Python & Pandas – KPI Implementation

After validating the KPIs in SQL, I implemented the calculations using Python and Pandas.

I focused on preparing the data, handling missing values, and grouping information by time periods and user roles to ensure accurate results.

These calculations supported the KPI analysis and visualisations presented in the Factsheet.

### 6. Power Apps – Functional Prototype

I developed a Power Apps prototype consisting of two main pages demonstrating complaint handling and data visibility.

Because the SQL database was stored locally, I used Excel as an alternative data source.

Although the application was not fully implemented, the prototype demonstrated how users could interact with the proposed solution and how information could move from input to overview.

### 7. Final Presentation & Demonstration

During the S2 event, I presented the final project deliverables and explained how the ERD, KPIs, database, data analysis, and Power Apps prototype connected.

I also demonstrated the application to communicate how the proposed solution could support the organisation's operational processes.

## Outcome

The final sprints connected business process improvement, database development, data analysis, and application prototyping into one coherent solution.

Although the system was not fully implemented due to limited team capacity and technical constraints, the deliverables demonstrated its core functionality and alignment with the client's requirements.

This phase strengthened my skills in SQL, Python, Pandas, Power Apps, and translating technical work into business value.
