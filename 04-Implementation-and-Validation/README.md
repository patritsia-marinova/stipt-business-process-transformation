# Implementation & Validation – STIPT

## Overview

During the final sprints of the STIPT Business Process Transformation project, I focused on bringing together the system design, data analysis, and prototyping activities to demonstrate how the proposed solution could work in practice.

Building on the final ERD and KPIs, I developed a database, analysed performance indicators, created interactive prototypes, and prepared business deliverables to demonstrate the solution's functionality and value.

## Implementation & Validation Activities

### 1. Interactive Figma Prototype

I designed an interactive Figma prototype to visualise the proposed application's interface, navigation, and functionality.

This helped communicate how users would interact with the system before development. During the client meeting, Harald responded positively to the prototype, confirming that the proposed interface aligned with his expectations.

### 2. Invoice – Excel

I created an Excel invoice template covering the Figma prototype and Power Apps development work.

Although the invoice was not automatically generated, it demonstrated how the project's technical deliverables could be presented in a structured, professional business document.

### 3. Business Balanced Scorecard (BBSC)

Using the KPIs defined during Sprint 3, I developed a Business Balanced Scorecard to connect operational performance with the organisation's broader business objectives.

The scorecard demonstrates how indicators such as complaint handling and system usage could support management decisions and long-term improvements.

### 4. Factsheet – KPI Analysis & Visualisation

I developed a Factsheet using Python and Pandas to calculate and visualise selected KPIs.

I prepared the data by checking dates, ratings, missing values, and grouping information according to the KPI definitions. This helped ensure that the analysis was reliable and that the visualisations communicated meaningful performance results.

### 5. SQL Database & KPI Validation

Based on the final ERD, I developed SQL CREATE and INSERT statements to structure and populate the database.

I also wrote SQL queries for all five KPIs, including calculations, percentage thresholds, and HIT/NOT HIT evaluations.

This allowed me to validate the database structure and KPI calculations before implementing the analysis in Python.

### 6. Python & Pandas – KPI Implementation

After validating the KPIs in SQL, I implemented their calculations using Python and Pandas.

I focused on data preparation, handling missing values, and correctly grouping information by time periods and user roles.

The results supported the KPI analysis and visualisations presented in the Factsheet.

### 7. Power Apps – Functional Prototype

I developed a focused Power Apps prototype consisting of two main pages demonstrating complaint handling and data visibility.

Because the SQL database was stored locally, I used Excel as the prototype's data source.

Although the application was not fully implemented, the prototype demonstrated how users could interact with the proposed solution and how information could move from input to overview.

### 8. Final Presentation & Demonstration

During the S2 event, I presented the final deliverables and explained how the ERD, KPIs, database, data analysis, and Power Apps prototype connected.

I also demonstrated the application to show how the proposed solution could support the organisation's operational processes.

## Outcome

The final sprints connected business process improvement, database development, data analysis, and application prototyping into one solution.

Although the system was not fully implemented due to limited team capacity and technical constraints, the deliverables demonstrated its core functionality and alignment with the client's requirements.

This phase strengthened my skills in SQL, Python, Pandas, Power Apps, Figma, and translating technical work into business value.
