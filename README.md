# STIPT – Business Process Transformation

## Project Overview

This project was completed during Semester 2 of my ICT & Business studies at Fontys University of Applied Sciences. The goal was to analyse STIPT's existing business processes, identify operational inefficiencies, and develop a digital solution to improve planning, scheduling, communication, and complaint handling.

The project combined business process analysis, solution design, data modelling, performance analysis, and application prototyping to demonstrate how technology can support operational efficiency and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Approach

The project followed four main phases:

1. **Business Analysis:** Analysing the current processes (IST), identifying bottlenecks and wastes, and investigating root causes using a fishbone diagram.
2. **Process Improvement:** Designing improved workflows (SOLL), conducting a GAP analysis, and defining user needs, responsibilities, and requirements.
3. **Solution & System Design:** Developing measurable KPIs and an Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) to support the proposed solution.
4. **Implementation & Validation:** Developing SQL and Python KPI analysis, business performance reports, and a Power Apps prototype to demonstrate the solution in practice.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Visual Paradigm:** BPMN and business process modelling
- **SQL:** Database development and KPI validation
- **Python (Pandas):** Data preparation, analysis, and KPI visualisation
- **Microsoft Power Apps:** Application prototyping
- **Microsoft Excel:** Data management and business documentation
- **Business Analysis:** IST, SOLL, GAP analysis, fishbone, requirements, and Business Balanced Scorecard
