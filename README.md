# STIPT – Business Process Transformation

## Project Overview

This project was completed during Semester 2 of my ICT & Business studies at Fontys University of Applied Sciences. The goal was to analyse STIPT's existing business processes, identify inefficiencies, and explore opportunities for process optimisation and digital transformation.

The project combined business analysis, process modelling, data analysis, and automation to develop improvements based on the organisation's needs.

## Project Approach

The project followed a structured business process transformation approach:

1. **Business Analysis:** Understanding the current situation (IST), identifying bottlenecks and wastes, and investigating root causes using a fishbone diagram.
2. **Process Improvement:** Developing the desired future situation (SOLL) and conducting a GAP analysis to identify the changes required.
3. **Requirements Analysis:** Defining business, user, and system requirements to support the proposed solution.
4. **Technical Implementation:** Exploring digital solutions using tools such as Power Apps, Power Automate, SQL, and Python.

## Tools & Technologies

- Visual Paradigm – BPMN and process modelling
- Power Apps and Power Automate – Process automation
- SQL and Python (Pandas) – Data analysis
- Business analysis – IST, SOLL, GAP analysis, fishbone, bottlenecks and requirements
